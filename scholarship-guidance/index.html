Adapt it, do not send it as it is.

Why it worked

  • The subject line does the workEvery credential is in the subject, so a professor sees the case before deciding whether to open anything.
  • The first sentence names the researchNo throat clearing and no flattery. It says which area, so the professor knows in one line whether to keep reading.
  • Every claim carries a linkEach line ends with proof. Nothing has to be taken on trust, and the whole thing stays readable in about twenty seconds.
  • It asks for one specific thingIt closes by asking for a meeting. A message with no clear ask is the easiest kind to leave unanswered.

Step 03 Then write to me

Send one complete message.

I get a lot of these, and the ones I can answer properly are the ones that do not need three rounds of questions first. Put it all in the first message and I can usually give you something specific straight away.

  • Your degree, university, and CGPA
  • Your test scores, and which ones you still need to take
  • What you have actually built or published, with links
  • Whether you are aiming at an MS or a PhD, and in what area
  • The countries and the intake you are targeting
  • The one thing you are most stuck on right now
