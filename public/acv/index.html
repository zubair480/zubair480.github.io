Research Projects and Systems

ActorRelay · 1st place, YC Haunted Hacks (team)Oct 2026

An agentic control layer on a simulated two-plant process unit. Every machine has a durable actor holding its memory and its own AI agent; a setpoint changes only if every connected machine votes yes while citing past runs, and a human has a 10-second veto. One refusal blocks the change with its evidence, the pump’s agent offers a safer target, and a rule learned in one plant is cited in the other.

Evaluation: 26 of 26 full-stack end-to-end checks on real actors, a real relay and real model calls. Crash testing with kill -9 passed 32 of 33 scenarios with no acknowledged write lost across 37 server kills. Stack: Terse durable actors, Agent Relay, OpenAI models, TypeScript, Bun, three.js.

MongoMation · 1st place, MongoDB Harness Engineering Hackathon (team)Oct 2026

A self-improving autonomous PLC engineer. An agent turns a plain-English machine specification into an IEC 61131-3 Structured Text program, compiles and deploys it to a real OpenPLC runtime, and tests it against a live Factory I/O plant on AWS. An independent grader with hidden tests decides pass or fail and drives a repair loop, while a meta-agent proposes harness changes behind a benchmark-gated keep or rollback step.

Results: best custom specification met 8 of 8 requirements and passed 14 of 14 public and hidden tests after one repair; 7 fully verified projects and 473 graded tests; a repeated specification fell from 290 s and $0.073 to 1 s and $0 by recalling a rig-proven program. Stack: MongoDB Atlas (change streams, transactions, hybrid vector and text search), Voyage AI, Claude, Raindrop, Modelence, OpenPLC, Factory I/O, TypeScript.

PlantAPI · 3rd place, Build an Agent Hackathon (team, with Ali Amjad)Oct 2026

An eleven-agent maintenance team for an industrial plant, all running on Agent37. An operator uploads a photo of a failure; agents triage it, check repair history, source the part, find an outage window and a qualified technician, resolve disagreements into one plan, tag every action for risk and lockout/tagout, and after one human approval open the work order, email the supplier, book the technician and verify the completion photo.

Result: photo to closed work order in about 11 minutes on real systems for $0.13 of agent compute. Stack: Agent37, OpenAI vision, Supabase, Monid, InstaCloud, Fiix, Odoo, Next.js.

RefundGuard · 1st place, The Executable WorldSep 2026

A release gate for AI agents that hold a wallet. Eleven adaptive scam personas attack a refund agent in a simulated store while three honest customers act as the control. Every ledger movement is replayed against policy in code, and an LLM judge’s findings count only when they quote the agent verbatim.

Result: version 1 leaked $310 and promised $709 in writing; by version 3, 0 of 11 attacks breached and every honest customer was served. Stack: Node.js, LLM judge, ledger replay, server-sent events.

Thermal Crusoe · 1st place ($5,000), Crusoe × The AI Conference (team of 4)Sep 2026

Built the three.js twin and owned the Next.js APIs, shared contracts and integration.

Evaluation: a 180-request evaluation of the Crusoe-hosted model passed every check at about 681 ms median latency. Stack: three.js, Next.js, Crusoe, Plaud.

Attest · 2nd place, Agent Native Builders Hackathon (solo)Aug 2026

Agent-to-agent vendor security review in place of static compliance questionnaires. A bank’s agent questions a vendor’s agent, which runs with separate credentials. Stack: Cotal.ai agents, Runtype.ai, JavaScript; four evidence collectors with judgments cached on the evidence digest.
